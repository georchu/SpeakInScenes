Privacy Policy

SpeakInScenes does not require an account, show ads, use tracking technology, or collect personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your saved scenes, saved expressions, recent history, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. SpeakInScenes does not upload this information to us.

Network requests

The app requests its public scene catalog from GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive ordinary technical request data, such as an IP address and user agent, under its own privacy practices. SpeakInScenes does not add advertising identifiers, account identifiers, or personal information to these requests.

Speech

SpeakInScenes uses Apple’s system speech synthesizer to read English text aloud. The app does not request microphone access and does not record your voice.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app reads Apple’s signed purchase status to unlock Premium. We do not receive your payment card details.

Children

SpeakInScenes is a general-audience education app and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. Email privacy questions to ixxzhu@gmail.com.

隐私政策摘要

SpeakInScenes 不要求注册账号，不显示广告，不使用跟踪技术，也不收集个人数据。收藏、最近浏览和应用偏好仅保存在设备本地。应用通过 GitHub Pages 获取公开场景目录；订阅由 Apple 处理。应用不请求麦克风权限，也不录制用户声音。如有问题，请联系 ixxzhu@gmail.com。
