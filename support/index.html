SpeakInScenes Support

Email ixxzhu@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or full payment details.

Premium does not appear

Open SpeakInScenes → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure the device uses the same Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Apple may take a short time to update the transaction.

The full library does not load

Check your internet connection and reopen the app. The bundled starter scenes remain available offline. Saved items are not removed by a temporary connection problem.

Audio does not play

Increase the media volume and make sure another app is not controlling audio output. SpeakInScenes uses the English voices included with iOS.

Cancel or manage a subscription

Open SpeakInScenes → Settings → Manage Subscription, or open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Billing and refunds are handled by Apple.

Content feedback

If an expression sounds unclear or does not fit its scene, email the scene title and the expression. Content corrections can be published without waiting for an app update.
